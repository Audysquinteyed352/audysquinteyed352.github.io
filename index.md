## 📝 Tips for better videos

Be specific with your prompts. Instead of saying "make an animation," provide detail about the color, the motion speed, and the text content. If you want a corporate look, include keywords like "minimalist" or "clean modern style." If you want something energetic, use words like "fast movement" or "dynamic motion." The software listens to your descriptive adjectives to adjust the final visual style.
